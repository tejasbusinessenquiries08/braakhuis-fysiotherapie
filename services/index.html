Physiotherapy Treatments & Rehabilitation Protocols

Every session at Braakhuis Fysiotherapie is a private, one-on-one consultation with a senior HCPC-registered clinician. We define measurable milestones for discharge rather than open-ended appointments.

Spine, Neck, Shoulders & Hips£75 / 45 minutes

Manual Joint & Soft Tissue Therapy

Hands-on joint mobilisation, soft tissue release, and spinal assessment to restore movement and reduce acute joint stiffness.

Key Indications:

  • •Acute neck stiffness and cervicogenic headaches
  • •Thoracic spine restriction from prolonged desk work
  • •Restricted shoulder elevation and capsule tightness
  • •Sacroiliac joint discomfort and pelvic girdle asymmetry

Clinical Approach:

  • →Specific joint accessory motion assessment
  • →Grade I to IV passive joint mobilisations
  • →Myofascial trigger point deactivation
  • →Immediate active range testing and home corrective drills
When NOT suitable: Undiagnosed fractures, acute rheumatoid flare-ups, severe osteoporosis, or radiating numbness accompanied by sudden muscle weakness.

Knees, Ankles, Hamstrings & Tendons£70 / 45 minutes

Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Objective force testing, progressive tendon loading, and return-to-sport criteria for runners, field athletes, and lifters.

Key Indications:

  • •Patellar and Achilles tendinopathy
  • •Grade 1 and 2 ankle ligament sprains
  • •Hamstring and groin strain rehabilitation
  • •Rotator cuff impingement in overhead athletes

Clinical Approach:

  • →Handheld dynamometer strength symmetry testing
  • →Heavy slow resistance (HSR) tendon loading protocols
  • →Plyometric rate-of-force progression
  • →Video gait and landing mechanics analysis
When NOT suitable: Suspected complete tendon ruptures (such as Thompson-positive Achilles rupture) or acute ligament tears requiring immediate orthopaedic consultation.

Post-Surgical Knees, Hips & Shoulders£90 / 60 minutes

Post-Operative Orthopaedic Recovery

Structured phase-based rehabilitation following joint arthroscopy, ACL reconstruction, or hip and knee replacement.

Key Indications:

  • •Anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) reconstruction (Phases 1–4)
  • •Total knee and total hip arthroplasty rehabilitation
  • •Shoulder subacromial decompression and labral repairs
  • •Lumbar microdiscectomy post-surgical reactivation

Clinical Approach:

  • →Surgical protocol timeline adherence and surgeon liaison
  • →Early passive and active-assisted range restoration
  • →Swelling management and neuromuscular quadriceps activation
  • →Closed-kinetic-chain functional strengthening
When NOT suitable: Patients with acute surgical site infections, unhealed surgical dehiscence, or active deep vein thrombosis (DVT) symptoms.

Cervical, Thoracic & Lumbar Spine£75 / 45 minutes

Spine & Posture Decompression

Targeted clinical protocols for mechanical lower back pain, sciatica, and postural tension developed from desk work.

Key Indications:

  • •Mechanical lower back pain and facet joint irritation
  • •Sciatica and nerve root sensitivity without motor deficit
  • •Postural fatigue between the shoulder blades
  • •Ergonomic work-station related spinal stiffness

Clinical Approach:

  • →Directional preference McKenzie assessment
  • →Segmental core stabiliser recruitment under load
  • →Thoracic spine extension and rotational drills
  • →Custom desk and seating ergonomic setup audit
When NOT suitable: Sudden onset loss of bowel or bladder control, saddle anaesthesia, or progressive bilateral leg weakness (seek emergency A&E immediately).

Whole Body & Nervous System£75 / 45 minutes

Persistent Pain & Movement Desensitisation

Graded movement exposure, nervous system down-regulation, and capacity building for pain persisting beyond normal tissue healing times.

Key Indications:

  • •Musculoskeletal discomfort lasting longer than 12 weeks
  • •Movement apprehension and fear of re-injury
  • •Fibromyalgia and non-specific chronic spinal tension
  • •Persistent neck stiffness after resolved whiplash

Clinical Approach:

  • →Pain neurophysiology education and goal setting
  • →Graded motor imagery and pacing strategies
  • →Low-threshold resistance training to rebuild confidence
  • →Sleep hygiene and nervous system regulation tracking
When NOT suitable: Individuals seeking quick passive cures or those unwilling to commit to progressive home movement protocols.

Hips, Ankles & Core Equilibrium£65 / 45 minutes

Mobility, Balance & Fall Prevention

Evidence-based strength and balance training designed to maintain independent walking, stair climbing, and confidence at home.

Key Indications:

  • •Age-related loss of walking confidence and balance unsteady
  • •Post-fall rehabilitation and confidence rebuilding
  • •Mild to moderate hip and knee osteoarthritis
  • •Difficulty rising from low armchairs or stairs

Clinical Approach:

  • →Validated Berg Balance Scale and Timed Up and Go (TUG) testing
  • →Sit-to-stand power and hip abductor strengthening
  • →Proprioceptive balance pad perturbations
  • →Safe transfer and practical home obstacle navigation
When NOT suitable: Uncontrolled cardiac arrhythmias, severe unmanaged vestibular crises, or acute medical instability.
