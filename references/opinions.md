## Opinions

### Most patients do not need long treatment packages
- **The number behind it:** 82% of our non-surgical patients discharge within 4 to 6 sessions once active movement begins.
- **Why we think it:** Passive table massage without progressive loading creates clinic dependence. Our role is to test your joint, give you 3 targeted movements to do at home, and verify your progress each fortnight.
- **Who disagrees and why they are not crazy:** High-frequency treatment chains selling 20-session upfront blocks. They argue weekly hands-on touch provides ongoing reassurance.
- **Use it in posts about:** Session frequency, treatment costs, what to expect at physiotherapy.
- **When it does NOT apply:** Major surgical reconstructions (e.g. ACL reconstruction or spinal fusion), which follow strict 16 to 24 week clinical tissue healing timelines.
