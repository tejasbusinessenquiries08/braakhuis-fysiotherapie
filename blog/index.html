What Causes Lower Back Pain in Females?

Lower back pain in women usually comes from joint or muscle strain, but hormonal cycles and pelvic conditions also play a part. Here is how to tell the difference.

Direct Answer:Lower back pain in females usually comes down to one of two things: a mechanical joint or muscle issue, or internal pain linked to your monthly cycle or pelvic organs. If your back hurts more when you bend, lift, or sit, it is almost certainly a joint or muscle problem. If your ache flares up around your period, feels deep inside your pelvis, or stays exactly the same no matter how you sit or stand, it may be linked to your hormones or reproductive system.

Neck pain reasons and when to see a doctor

Most neck ache comes from everyday muscle tightness, joint stiffness, or sleeping in an awkward position. Here is how to tell common neck ache from something that needs medical care.

Direct Answer:Most neck pain reasons come down to simple mechanical strain. This happens when the muscles, ligaments, or small hinge joints in your neck become irritated from holding one position too long, sleeping awkwardly, or sudden movement. For most people, a stiff neck eases within one to two weeks with gentle daily movement and warmth. You only need urgent medical care if neck ache comes with numbness down your arm, severe headaches, fever, or clumsiness in your hands.
